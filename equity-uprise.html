What Equity Uprise is

A movement down the middle — and out the other side

The two-party fight keeps people divided and answering to the same central forces. Equity Uprise walks straight down the middle of the aisle and out the other side: a platform built to organize people around what they actually need, not which team they're told to pick.

Culture as organizing power

We organize with music and art as political activism — concerts, events, and content that educate and move people. Every anthem in the McCluster catalogue doubles as a tool: a song that carries a policy, a story, a call to act.

Your talent is the superpower

Whatever you're great at is what the movement needs. The Policy Fellowship is free — you tell the movement who you are, what you can do, and what you'd fix first. Your first act as a fellow is a vote.

Policy practice

The memo comes before the microphone.

Equity Uprise is not only advocacy creative. The policy layer is designed for actual staff review: source records, administrative routing, measurable standards, implementation questions, and clear lines between recommendations and legal/technical review.

Prepared August 11, 2026 for legislative record and staff use. The memo asks DeKalb to regulate measurable operating performance rather than rely only on pre-construction paperwork, with routing to Planning & Sustainability, County Law, Watershed Management, and Board policy staff.

Campaign operating model

Research it. Write it. Show up. Make culture. Measure it.

For a live issue, Equity Uprise can operate from the first stakeholder map through the final public record. The engagement can be narrow or full-stack, but the work stays auditable.

Research

Source record, law/policy landscape, local history, comparable jurisdictions, issue framing.

Stakeholder map

Decision-makers, residents, operators, advocates, subject-matter experts, creators, media and institutional voices.

Policy memo

Clear recommendations, technical questions, draftable language, provenance, and implementation considerations.

Public advocacy

Meetings, hearings, testimony support, briefings, coalition coordination and public-record engagement.

Culture + media

Artists, original music, creator campaigns, explainers, film/photo, interviews and short-form distribution.

Capstone + record

Public event/listening session, analytics, after-action report, publication and a durable archive of the work.

Three campaign lanes

One issue. More than one honest audience.

Equity Uprise can produce distinct sponsored public-education lanes around the same controversy while preserving sponsor disclosure and an independent research layer.

Lane 01

Responsible growth

For operators, builders, utilities, economic-development groups, suppliers, labor and investors making the affirmative case for infrastructure with measurable safeguards.

Lane 02

Community protection

For neighborhood, environmental, ratepayer, water, land-use and public-interest organizations focused on siting, burden, accountability and continuing compliance.

Lane 03

Public inquiry

For universities, foundations, civic-information organizations and institutions that want the questions, evidence and competing claims surfaced clearly.
